Affiliate disclosure

SpecWise may earn a commission when you follow certain retailer links and make a qualifying purchase, at no extra cost to you where the program works that way. Affiliate relationships will be labelled clearly. They must not determine our comparison scores or verdicts.

This is a draft disclosure for the prototype. Before launch, update it to accurately name the affiliate programs actually joined and meet each program's required wording.

Privacy notice — draft

This prototype does not currently submit search terms to a server or include analytics, advertising cookies, user accounts, or payment processing. Search runs locally in your browser.

Before launch, this notice must be updated to describe the real hosting provider, server logs, contact form (if added), analytics, advertising partners, cookies or similar technologies, what information is collected, purposes and lawful basis, retention, recipients, cross-border transfers where relevant, and how people can exercise their rights under South Africa's POPIA. Do not treat this draft as legal advice or publish it unchanged if the site's behaviour changes.

Terms — draft

Comparison information is intended to help users research choices, not to replace professional advice. Specifications, availability, warranties and prices can change; users should confirm details with the seller or service provider before purchasing.

Before launch, have the terms reviewed and adapt them to the actual business, data sources, product categories, dispute process, liability rules and applicable South African law.
